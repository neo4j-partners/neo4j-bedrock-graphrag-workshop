= Part 1: Getting Started with Neo4j Aura and the Dataset

Part 1 gets everyone connected and oriented on the data in under 30 minutes. You will sign up for a Neo4j Aura free trial, load the SEC 10-K financial knowledge graph, and explore it with Cypher and Neo4j Explore. There is no AWS Marketplace walkthrough here: everyone uses the Neo4j Aura free trial.

By the end of Part 1 you will have a running knowledge graph, loaded with both structured entities and the unstructured document layer, ready for the GraphRAG labs that follow.
