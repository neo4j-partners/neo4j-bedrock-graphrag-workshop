= Workshop Slides

Five presentation decks accompany this workshop. They provide the conceptual background behind each part: the AWS and Neo4j partnership, graph and GraphRAG foundations, retriever patterns, and agents with MCP. Presenters can walk through them before the hands-on labs, and self-paced attendees can read them for the theory behind the code.

The decks are published as a standalone slide gallery. Open any deck below, or browse the full https://neo4j-partners.github.io/neo4j-bedrock-graphrag-workshop/slides/[slide gallery].
